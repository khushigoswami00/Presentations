4. Reading Rabindranath Tagore’s The Home and the World and T. P. Kailasam’s The Curse or Karna, Applying New Historicism to Tagore’s The Home and the World demonstrates that the novel simultaneously mirrors and critiques the Swadeshi movement by dramatizing the clash between Sandip’s militant nationalism and Nikhil’s ethical humanism. The text reveals that nationalist fervor, when divorced from moral responsibility, risks replicating the same structures of domination it opposes. Thus, Tagore’s narrative does not merely reflect its historical moment but actively intervenes in it, positioning humanism as a necessary counterbalance to political fanaticism.
