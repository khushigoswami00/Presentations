Whittle’s article suggests that both Caute’s novel and Fanon’s theory diagnose the same structural danger of independence — that colonial hierarchies may survive through a new national elite. By combining literary criticism with postcolonial and Marxist theories, Whittle argues that fiction and political thought together offer a fuller understanding of decolonization than either text alone.
